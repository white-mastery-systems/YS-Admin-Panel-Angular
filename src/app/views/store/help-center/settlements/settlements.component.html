Settlements

  1. Go to Managements → Settlements
  2. Here you can find the list of the orders that are due settlement or have been settled.
  3. Here you can find the date when the amount will be settled to your account for the respective orders.
  4. Under value you would find two amounts Example: 105/110. Here 110 is the original value of the order and 105 is the settlement amount to you after the charges are deducted.
  5. Status Paid denotes you have been settled and Pending denotes the amount is yet to be settled.
  6. Click on View to get more details about the settlement charges.
