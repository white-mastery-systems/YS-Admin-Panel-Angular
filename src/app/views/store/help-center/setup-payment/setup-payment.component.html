Setup Payment

  1. Go to your Yourstore Dashboard and Go to Settings → Payment Gateway
  2. You can choose to use Gpay and COD that are readily available by enabling it.
  3. To collect online payment through 100+ methods like debit/credit card, net banking, UPI, wallets, EMI, etc.. select Razorpay (INDIA alone) as payment option by clicking the Add New option available and click on Create Razorpay Account.
  4. Click on View Steps and follow the steps mentioned.
  5. Business from other countries, please choose the relevant payment gateway supported in your region/country.
