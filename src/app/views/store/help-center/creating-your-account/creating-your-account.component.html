Creating your account

Signup

  1. Click on Start selling on the top navigation bar of the homepage.
  2. Complete the fields with your Name, Mobile Number, Email ID and your Password.
  3. Click "Create Account" and fillup with the other basic details requested for store creation.

Verify your E-Mail

  1. A verification email will be sent to your email for store activation. Open the email you used to register your account on Yourstore and click on the link received to activate.
  2. Once the activation link is clicked, you can proceed to login, access your Yourstore Dashboard and start using.

    3. If you haven't received an email, please check your Spam/Junk folder. Remember to mark "Not Spam" this email so it can be delivered to your Inbox when we send future updates. If your unable to locate the email in spam/junk or inbox, click this link and enter the Email Id & Password used to create your account to receive the email once again.
