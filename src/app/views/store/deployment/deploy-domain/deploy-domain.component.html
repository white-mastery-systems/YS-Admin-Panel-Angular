To add an A record and a CNAME record to your domain’s DNS records, you need to follow these steps:

  • Sign in to your domain host account and find where you manage your DNS settings. This may vary depending on your domain host provider.
  • To add an A record, click Add Record and select A Record as the record type. Enter @ as the name and 151.106.7.18 as the value. Set the TTL (time to live) to 3600 or leave it as default. Please make sure that there are no other A Records apart from this. If so, please delete the other A records.
  • To add a CNAME record, click Add Record and select CNAME Record as the record type. Enter www as the name and {{domainForm.domain}}. as the value. Set the TTL to 3600 or leave it as default. (Add the dot as shown above in the end of your domain name)
  • Save the changes.
  • Once the above steps are complete click on the “Validate Connection” button below and your domain will be linked within 24-48 hours.
